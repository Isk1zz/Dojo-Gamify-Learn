## QUEUED 2026-11-24 — final asks: a balloon, a plane, a rocket

Asked as the last items for the stack. Not started. All three live in the
lobby's sky, next to the existing decorations (shop/decor.js, the sky in
styles/base.css, sounds in core/sfx.js), and like every sky effect they
skip their animation under "reduce motion" (spawn() already does).

### 1. A hot-air balloon that bursts into a celebration

Asked: «a baloon that you can tap and cabin disappears and from it sulte and
firewoks fall (sfx vfx) i want it colorful and nice». A balloon drifts
across the sky. Tapped, its basket (the cabin) vanishes, and out of it fall
confetti («салют») and fireworks: bursts of colour that open and drift
down. Colourful and pleasant to watch, with its own synthesized sounds
(pops, crackle, a whistle). The balloon comes back later, the way a downed
bird returns.

### 2. A plane, and a rocket

Asked: «add a plane and a rocket (less frequent than the birds obv) to a
plane add a peace sign plane name "da plane"». Two fly-bys, rarer than the
birds. The plane carries a peace sign ☮ and its name, "da plane". The
rocket crosses the sky with a trail. Making them clickable (a sound at
least) is a natural next step, but it was not asked: ask first.
