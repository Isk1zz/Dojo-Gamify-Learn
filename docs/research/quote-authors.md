# Quote pool — approved author candidates

Working list for the ×5 expansion of `library/content/quotes.js`, which holds
**58 quotes by 32 authors** today. Approved by the user on 2026-08-24 ("аппрув").

**95 candidates.** At roughly 2–3 quotes each that overshoots the ×5 target
(~290), which is deliberate — the list is meant to survive his filtering.

## Rules that govern this list

- **No communist or Soviet figures.** Standing constraint, stated as
  *«никакого коммунизма!»*. Tolstoy, Dostoevsky and Chekhov are included as
  pre-1917 and that was flagged to him explicitly when the list was given.
- **Every quote keeps the pool's `verified` discipline.** 49 of the current 58
  are sourced to an edition; 9 carry `verified: false` because the primary
  source was not located. A new quote gets a real citation or it gets the flag.
- **Goodreads is not a source.** Cicero in particular attracts fabrications —
  the "budget should be balanced" line is from a 1965 novel by Taylor Caldwell
  and circulates everywhere as his.
