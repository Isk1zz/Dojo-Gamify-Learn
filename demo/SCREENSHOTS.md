# Knell demo — screenshots and a 60-second recording

All four screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/`. They were taken from the demo
build on its sample profile ("Sunshine"), at a 1200 × 800 window.

## The four screenshots

| File | Screen | State | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| `demo-lobby.jpg` | Lobby | Just after **Begin**, first-run tour skipped | Opens with no sign-in. A real student state: rank *Lab Intern*, most of the way to the next, a 9-day streak, "Continue where you left off". The demo banner is visible. |
| `demo-course-scene.jpg` | Inside the e-bike course | Chapters 1–5 done, 6 next | A course drawn as a place. Progress shows on the scene itself: finished chapters ticked, the next highlighted, the runner standing at it. |
| `demo-garden.jpg` | Garden | e-bike course expanded | Spaced review made visible: one topic at every growth stage (blossom, tree, growing, seedling, sprout, empty), and "3 plants need watering". |
| `demo-lesson.jpg` | Lesson | Resumed from **Continue**, chunk 2 of 3 | The app remembers where you stopped, and a lesson is a real explanation with sections, not a flashcard. |

To retake them: open the demo in a private window (a fresh visit gets the
sample profile), choose English, set the window to about 1200 × 800. A
fresh visit covers the rank with a sticker: rub it for 7 seconds (or hold
Enter on it) and close the guide that follows, so the top bar shows the rank.

## 60-second screen recording

One caption per step, shown at the bottom of the frame.

Every step below was clicked through on the demo build. The order is chosen
so that the review screen's own back buttons lead straight into the course.

| Time | Do | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the demo link, choose **English** | *Knell: a study app. This is the demo, no account needed.* |
| 0:06 | Click **Begin Training**, then **Skip** on the tour | *A sample student, mid-course: rank, streak, where they stopped.* |
| 0:12 | Click **Museum**, then **← Lobby** | *The Forum is built, not launched yet. Here it is an exhibit.* |
| 0:18 | Click **Garden** | *Every passed topic is a plant. Its size is its review interval.* |
| 0:26 | Click **Water the first →** | *Reviews come back on a schedule. Three are due.* |
| 0:34 | Click **← Topics**, then **← Units** | *A course is a place. Ticked chapters are done.* |
| 0:42 | Click chapter **7 · Your signs** and let the runner arrive | *Pick a chapter: the runner crosses at a zebra, then it opens.* |
| 0:54 | Hold on the lesson | *Designed and directed by me. Code written with AI (Claude).* |

Record in a fresh private window, so the visit starts on the sample profile
with the runner at chapter 6.
